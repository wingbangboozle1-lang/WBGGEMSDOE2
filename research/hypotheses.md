## Scientific basis and limits

The [USGS Argenta Rise study](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/70271418) combines gravity, magnetic and MT observations for a possible blind geothermal system; it does **not** validate H1's proposed shift rule. The [USGS play-fairway synthesis](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/discovering-blind-geothermal-systems-great-basin-region-integrated-geologic-and) describes blind systems identified by multiple observations. Geothermal favorability is not equivalent to the competition's fault-label target; a predicted structure is not evidence of a vent. The [GeoDAWN release](https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and) notes varying terrain clearance and survey specifications, so acquisition direction and relief are necessary confounders.

## Fixed gates before any future attempt

1. First resolve the registry policy **without silently exempting** the lattice or continuous prior, restore the actual shared feature bank, and authenticate the grid/source rights. Do not move dots solely to defeat an audit.
2. On a proposed surface **before placement** compare exact footprint rank correlation and preview-dot overlap against every member of a complete, immutable registry; repeat on final dots. A single correlation >0.90 or overlap >70% is STOP.
3. For each feature on each buffered, whole-segment holdout, use only visible-fault-derived catalogue features; AUC >0.90 (or its two-sided inverse) is a leakage alarm. Mask visible faults pixel-exactly. The full-catalogue `geo_f32` / `dtip_f32` in the template preparation script must not be reused within a held-out fold.
4. Use the unmodified [shared template](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/57GEMSDOE/tree/822a0ce5546c7b6749cdd82eb145b6b3e616603d) `evaluate_holdout.py` and `submission_writer.py`; pool DTI components with α=0.2, β=0.8 and a 300 m triangular kernel. Report evaluator version, withheld positives and 95% spatial-block CI. Rerun the incumbent on the **same** folds and budget. Only a paired improvement with a positive lower bound can pass science; a mapped-fault proxy is not a calibrated leaderboard score.
5. A format pass, negative experiment, or a related-site approval is not a slot approval. Selector and current authenticated quota are separate. At most three experiments or two hours per campaign.
