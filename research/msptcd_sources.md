## Band metadata caveat

In the owner mirror, the band-6 description says tilt angle or total curvature, while the organizer page illustrates a radiometric `tc` layer. [Reproducible mirror measurements](../evidence/input_metadata_20261027.json) find 3,073 masked sentinels and 5,164,300 valid cells with no negative valid values. That does not establish band identity or units. The depth band's official description says **conductive-base depth**; the mirror calls it basement depth, which is not sufficient evidence to reinterpret it as geological basement. No authenticated metadata has reconciled either disagreement.

See [corrected MSPTCD findings](msptcd_findings.md) and [new hypotheses](hypotheses.md) for the distinction between physical hypotheses, measurements, and results.
