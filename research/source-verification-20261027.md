## Why B2 might have performed well, and what cannot be inferred

The hash-pinned, owner-mirrored rasters show that GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 have identical in-footprint masks. GEMSDOE32 B2 equals that D2.8 mask with points at Euclidean pixel-center distance ≤2 pixels from the mirrored fault catalogue removed: 44,090 → 37,654 points. GEMSDOE36 adds 1,200 points to B2 without removing any. See the independently re-opened [registry audit](../evidence/registry_audit.json). These are array relationships, not independently verified submission receipts.

The official metric gives each true fault pixel the maximum distance-weighted contribution from nearby predictions, while prediction mass away from truth contributes false positives. Therefore, thinning redundant nearby predictions **can** preserve coverage while lowering false-positive mass; it can also remove a useful unique prediction and lower true-positive coverage. Exact known-fault pixels are masked by the organizers, but no additional pruning buffer is confirmed. Hidden labels, the actual receipt linking B2's bytes to the **USER-REPORTED 0.2778**, and per-pixel counterfactual contributions are unavailable. Thus the causal reason for the claim is unproven, and there is no defensible estimate that a proposed new file will beat it or the public leader.

## Index scope and provenance

A hash-checked [shared owner registry index](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/57GEMSDOE/blob/822a0ce5546c7b6749cdd82eb145b6b3e616603d/evidence/registry_refreshed.json) contains 695 unique raster-hash rows dated 2026-10-10. Of those, 684 hash rows are absent from this repo's 13-file witness manifest. We counted **metadata only**, not those 684 raster bytes; later, private or unlinked entries are also possible. [Index summary](../evidence/shared_registry_index_summary.json). A counterexample is enough to stop, but this old index cannot certify a current exhaustive pass.
