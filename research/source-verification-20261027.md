## Why B2 might have performed well, and what cannot be inferred

The hash-pinned, owner-mirrored rasters show that GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 have identical in-footprint masks. GEMSDOE32 B2 equals that D2.8 mask with points at Euclidean pixel-center distance ≤2 pixels from the mirrored fault catalogue removed: 44,090 → 37,654 points. GEMSDOE36 adds 1,200 points to B2 without removing any. See the independently re-opened [registry audit](../evidence/registry_audit.json). These are array relationships, not independently verified submission receipts.

The official metric gives each true fault pixel the maximum distance-weighted contribution from nearby predictions, while prediction mass away from truth contributes false positives. Therefore, thinning redundant nearby predictions **can** preserve coverage while lowering false-positive mass; it can also remove a useful unique prediction and lower true-positive coverage. Exact known-fault pixels are masked by the organizers, but staff explicitly confirmed no surrounding buffer, and new truth may lie within 300 m. Hidden labels, the actual receipt linking B2's bytes to the **USER-REPORTED 0.2778**, and per-pixel counterfactual contributions are unavailable. Thus the causal reason for the claim is unproven, and there is no defensible estimate that a proposed new file will beat it or the public leader.

## New desk-proposal source checks and prior-work correction

A [pinned, README-only scan of the nine focus repositories](focus-head-readme-review-20261027.md) found that GEMSDOE32 reports ARENA49-PCG phase congruency and GEMSDOE36 reports an N2 K/Th alteration experiment. We withdrew those ideas from the prospective list. GEMSDOE28 and GEMSDOE24 also document related texture/drainage work; the revised proposals disclose overlap and do not establish fleet-wide novelty. No sibling holdout was replayed.

- [USGS aeromagnetic processing report](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2013/1024/i/index_.html) describes magnetic-gradient boundaries that can locally coincide with and extend faults under cover. The [full report](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2013/1024/i/downloads/ofr2013-1024_i.pdf) discusses upward continuation; it does not validate P1's ridge-trajectory proposal or predict DTI.
- [USGS ComCat event API documentation](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/fdsnws/event/1/) documents focal-mechanism and moment-tensor product filters, and event-specific associated products. A target-area event count or actual API access was not tested. GEMSDOE32 also proposed ComCat-related work as data-blocked.
- [USGS modern water-data APIs](https://api.waterdata.usgs.gov/) describe public groundwater data; [USGS guidance](https://waterdata.usgs.gov/blog/api-whats-new-wdfn-apis/) notes a free API key may be needed for more than a handful of requests per hour. [USGS groundwater research](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/sir20125261) explains that head discontinuities may reflect faults **or other barriers**. We did not test exact well records, local aquifer coverage, data bytes or sandbox access.
