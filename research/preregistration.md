## Frozen scientific plan, conditional on passing preflight

- Budget: at most three scientific experiments or two hours; no portal submission and no selector decision. This session has not run a scientific experiment.
- Candidate dot budget: 37,654 distinct footprint pixels, matching the historical GEMSDOE32 B2 raster's independently checked support count. This is a comparison design choice, not a claimed optimum or score.
- Use the shared GEMSDOE24 template and its cached label-free feature stack, `scripts/evaluate_holdout.py` and `scripts/submission_writer.py`; do not fork the evaluator or writer. Verify their pinned bytes. Any catalogue-derived input must be constructed afresh from each fold's visible segments only.
- Check the full, current, hash-verified literal registry before placement and after placement. Stop on absolute surface/final rank correlation greater than 0.90 or directional final/preview overlap greater than 70% within 3 pixels of **any** registry raster. A density-based exemption is not authorized. A witness is enough for a fail, but not a pass.
- Each feature receives a standalone leakage canary; AUC greater than 0.90 is treated as leakage until disproved. In a valid run, withhold whole fault components/segments with a buffer, mask visible faults pixel-exactly, and pool TP/FP/FN using the organizer's 300 m triangular DTI with alpha 0.2 and beta 0.8. Give evaluator version, number of withheld positives, and a 95% interval; compare paired uncertainty with a same-protocol, same-budget incumbent. No leaderboard projection is a score.
- Do not train or place dots if a preflight mathematical bound already proves the literal gate must fail. Do not move dots to evade that bound. A negative result is a deliverable. The preflight and its limitations are documented in the run card.
