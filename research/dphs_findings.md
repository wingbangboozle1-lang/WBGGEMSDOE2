# DPHS campaign — historical evidence and independent recheck

**Decision: NEGATIVE / DO NOT SUBMIT.** No new candidate, model experiment, dot placement or competition upload was made in this review. The current method lane is **UNASSIGNED**, and the 13 pinned witnesses do not constitute a complete current registry. The original DPHS launcher and fleet/score scripts are retired; their unmodified source and former claims are preserved under [`evidence/retired_20261027/`](../evidence/retired_20261027/).

## What the source reports, and what was not replayed

The historical [`wbg2_dphs_results.json`](../evidence/wbg2_dphs_results.json) reports **HOLDOUT-DTI 0.173477**, 95% CI **[0.152451, 0.194361]**, 60,988 withheld positives, evaluator `gems57-pooled-hide-v2`; its cited `msptcd_31` comparator is **HOLDOUT-DTI 0.029605**, 95% CI **[0.019642, 0.040657]**, with the same evaluator and withheld-positive count. The reported interval is conditional on the historical fitted folds and budgets. The campaign, 61 feature canaries, original surface and full 467-raster audit were **not independently rerun** here. A canary AUC at or below 0.90 is a necessary screen, not proof of no leakage. The result is neither an organizer receipt nor a leaderboard forecast and does not validate corrected MSPTCD v2.

The archived runner was unsafe to reuse: it constructed and placed the final dots, deleted the contents of the historical DPHS download folder, and wrote a TIFF before a pre-placement all-registry check. Its full-map surface mixed fitted full-map predictions with out-of-fold predictions. Those facts do not establish why its score or overlap occurred, but they rule out treating the old launcher as a protocol-compliant release path. The old 467-raster source reports correlations and an empirical score model using user-reported claims; the full inventory and model were not independently validated, and the model's estimates are not accepted as scores or forecasts.

## Corrections to the previous narrative

The [official problem description](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) and [organizer clarification](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516/2) support masking exact known-fault pixels, not the whole surrounding two-pixel collar. A nearby dot is not automatically a false positive: deleting it might reduce false-positive weight or lose hidden-truth coverage. No causal explanation of B2's reported 0.2778, guarantee of exceeding it, or guaranteed path above the public leader is established.

The prior DPHS writeup also claimed several fleet scores and an exact 0.3133 for GEMSDOE70 that conflict with the supplied historical ledger; those claims and its forecast are not accepted as evidence. A historical ledger entry is not verified merely because a GitHub repository exists. The 2026-10-27 ledger and conflict register contain different snapshots; a filename/score mismatch must not be silently joined. The public leaderboard observation of 0.4544 is dated and organizer-published, not a project-file receipt.

The historical external-layer fetcher used a mutable tag and a manifest fetched from that tag as the expected hash source for some DEM channels. Its recorded GeoDAWN DOI also differs from the [official USGS release DOI](https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and). A hash match to an owner-derived mirror does not authenticate derivation from that official release or its license. The old fetcher is retired without altering the historical provenance receipt; future use requires immutable pins and independent source/license verification.

The prior prose asserted that exterior NaNs caused the reported portal error and that all-finite exterior values prevent rejection. Neither is established without the rejected bytes and an actual organizer response. The official page calls for null/NaN outside bounds; the optional stricter all-finite diagnostic is not proof of compliance or acceptance. The unchanged shared template's interior NoData-mask defect remains open; see the [reproducer](shared-template-format-report.md).

## Next scientifically defensible step

The current, qualitative [N1–N4 proposals](hypotheses.md) identify named layers, non-fault mimics, access caveats and disconfirmation criteria. They are unimplemented and do not assign a lane. Resolve the registry policy and coverage, authenticate the mirrored inputs, fix the shared validator upstream, and assign one method before a candidate experiment. Any future run must check the original surface **before** placement and the final dots, evaluate every feature alone for leakage on buffered whole-segment folds, use only fold-visible catalogue features and pixel-exact masking, and compare against a matched incumbent with the shared pooled DTI and paired spatial CI. Only a separate authorized selector can choose a weekly slot.
