## Flagged irregularities (for human review)

1. **Leaderboard values in the brief are stale/inconsistent.** The request says
   "0.3195 is the highest score right now" and elsewhere 0.4413; the official
   leaderboard fetched 2026-10-27 shows 0.4544 at #1. A public leaderboard row is
   not a receipt for any of our files.
2. **Sample-submission irregularity persists.** The mirrored
   `sample_submission.tif` (SHA-256 `2176d08e…`) has exactly 60,988 positive cells,
   identical to the mirrored existing-fault catalogue, whereas the official page
   describes a sample predicting total fault absence. The grid is hash-pinned but
   not organizer-authenticated.
3. **No DrivenData authentication.** `training_features.tif`, `labels.tif`,
   `sample_submission.tif` and `1m_DEM_links.csv` cannot be downloaded from the
   official data page in this sandbox (login required). All inputs are
   owner-mirrored, hash-checked public GitHub blobs.
4. **Two USGS pages failed on fetch:** the blind-fault FAQ returned HTTP 403 and an
   external-grant PDF (G16AP00141) returned 404. Claims relying on them were moved
   to reachable USGS sources (S5, S6).
5. **Registry incompleteness.** 8 of 126 ledger rows could not be resolved to a
   TIFF (blank submission names, renamed files, one >12 MB binary). HEAD-tree
   blobs are unpinned snapshots. See `evidence/registry_expanded.json`.
6. **Literal duplicate rule is mathematically unsatisfiable** against the
   registered lattice/continuous prior. The two-reading policy decision is in
   `docs/PROJECT_CHARTER.md`; both readings are published.
7. **Portal `[0,1]` error** reported by the user cannot be diagnosed without the
   exact rejected TIFF. Our writer rejects NaN/Inf and out-of-range values before
   writing; it does not silently clip.
