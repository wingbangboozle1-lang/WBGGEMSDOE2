The distal TIFF's lattice overlap is below threshold; its failing witness is the continuous prior, whose interpretation remains unresolved. This is a forensic rejection, not a new experiment or complete registry pass.
- The [independent registry rerun](../evidence/registry_independent_review_20261027.json) re-fetches 13 pinned witnesses and reproduces B2 = D2.8 pruned at 2 px; GEMSDOE25/30 decoded identity; and GEMSDOE36's 1,200 additions. User-reported historical numbers are not organizer receipts.
- The lattice covers **5,160,724 / 5,167,373 = 99.8713%** of footprint cells at an inclusive 3 px, **not 99.96%** as a prior lane wrote. Only 6,649 positions escape it. At 37,654 distinct dots the minimum possible overlap is 82.3418%. This is a counting bound, not a score or a proof about every smaller budget. The continuous prior blocks every nonempty candidate under the stated finite-positive support definition; no exemption is silently applied.
- The restored feature source is **418,912,844 bytes**, SHA-256 `4371c82e3b8339b807bdffcf4ef59a225520fe2988d521be208ae33743123bc5`, with 19 raw caches. A prior README claimed a different size/hash without a matching campaign input receipt. This review does not authenticate either mirror to organizer bytes.
