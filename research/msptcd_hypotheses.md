# MSPTCD historical features and the new proposal shortlist

The prior campaign **actually implemented** potential-field tilt/horizontal gradients, dip-shifted gravity/magnetic concordance, conductive-flank asymmetry and multiscale Hessian scarp features. Therefore the older depth-shift and flank-asymmetry ideas are not described as untried in this repository. Their implementation does not establish scientific novelty or absence of leakage.

The archived campaign selected among three experiments on the same mapped-fault proxy folds, then encountered the literal duplicate gate. It is **not a new candidate**, and its diagnostic detached-splay split does not establish independence from fitted-model training. The campaign exhausted its three-experiment budget. See [corrected findings](msptcd_findings.md) and the [run card](../evidence/RUN_CARD_MSPTCD.json).

For 3–5 distinctly formulated, clearly **untested proposals**, their layers, plausible signatures, non-fault mimics, qualitative potential/cost ranking and external source access limitations, see [the current structured shortlist](hypotheses.md). It is not a method-lane assignment. Fleet-wide novelty is unverified; newer priority-site rasters were not audited.

**Band 6 caution:** The original campaign used the variable name `tc_radiometric` based on an interpretation of the mirror. The physically correct identity and units of this band are unresolved, not proven by nonnegative valid values. See [metadata read-back](../evidence/input_metadata_20261027.json).
