# Project working agreement

At the start of every session read `README.md`, `docs/PROJECT_CHARTER.md`, `research/repo-review-20261029.md`, `research/session-review-20261029.md`, `research/findings.md`, `evidence/run_card.json`, `evidence/release_audit.json`, `evidence/checkout_readiness.json`, `evidence/session_run_card_20261029.json`, and `research/repo-review-20261028.md`. The nine requested historical submissions and their evidence labels are in `docs/executive-summary.html` and `research/focus-sites-review-20261028.md`. The latest checkout status is **BLOCKED / DO NOT SUBMIT**; rerun `python scripts/check_readiness.py --check` at session start because ignored assets and leaderboard snapshots can change. `evidence/RUN_CARD_WBG4.json`, `evidence/RUN_CARD_WBG4_EDTI.json`, `evidence/RUN_CARD_DISTAL_OOF.json`, and `config/release_register.json` preserve historical research records, not upload authority. Treat older README sections and old “ready to submit” language as archived context only.

- No new TIFF or real competition slot without the specified research protocol and a separate selector decision. Do not generate a candidate when the lane, required data/evaluator, or registry checks are blocked.
- Enforce the literal duplicate stop against every registry raster before placement and on final dots. An incomplete registry cannot prove uniqueness. The support of a registered continuous probability prior is ambiguous under the dot rule: do not silently exempt it.
- Use only an explicitly assigned method lane and the unchanged pinned shared template; if the lane is missing, stop and record the blocker rather than self-assigning a ranked proposal. Fix shared tools upstream, not in a private fork. Derive catalogue features from visible faults only for each spatial holdout, and run single-feature leakage canaries.
- Do not call a leaderboard projection or user claim an organizer receipt. Label observed leaderboard values, holdout scores, evaluators, positive counts and confidence intervals accurately. Do not silently repair candidate values.
- Keep data and large generated assets out of Git; document sources, rights, hashes, limitations, stale states and negative results.
- If evidence, data access or policy blocks completion, say so and fail closed instead of fabricating a candidate.

Current publication authority is negative-only. `src/wbggems/release.py` separates hash-bound registered research assets from `approved_candidates()`. Registration, format validation, holdout evidence and uniqueness evidence do not authorize a weekly slot. `approved_candidates()` requires a separate selector receipt file whose SHA-256 and candidate binding validate and whose recorded remaining quota is positive; no such receipt exists in this checkout. Keep all current run-card aliases equal and do not restore archived approvals.
