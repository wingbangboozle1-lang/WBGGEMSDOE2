PROVISIONAL HYPOTHESES

Four directions worth testing

Full preregistration ↗

Ranked by qualitative expected usefulness and implementation cost—not by a measured or projected score. No explicit method lane was assigned in this checkout.

01

Magnetic displacement × topography

PROVISIONAL LEAD · MEDIUM COST

Compare cross-scale offsets in reduced-to-pole magnetic edges with independent detrended topographic steps. It may reveal covered structures missed by surface maps.

Non-fault mimic: lithologic contacts or survey-leveling artifacts. The restored mirror contains the relevant bands; no model was built.

02

Flight-line-aware magnetic persistence

MEDIUM COST

Use magnetic edges plus survey flight paths to discount acquisition-aligned artifacts and seek corroboration from gravity or topography.

Non-fault mimic: microleveling artifacts and ordinary contacts. Official survey data are listed, but raw coverage and processing are unverified here.

03

Shallow heat × structural edges

HIGH COST

Study sparse 2 m probe anomalies in relation to independent magnetic or topographic features, explicitly accounting for uneven sampling.

Non-fault mimic: solar heating, groundwater, and sampling bias. The public source is listed, but its bytes and coverage are not verified in this run.

04

Scarp–radiometric contact offsets

HIGH COST

Compare 1 m topographic steps with independently mapped radiometric boundaries to distinguish a potential displacement from a single-layer lineament.

Non-fault mimic: terraces, roads, and lithologic contacts. Competition DEM links and exact tile coverage are not verified here.

DATA READINESS

Restored, with provenance limits.

The existing shared preparation script restored a 19-band feature stack and cached the bands from a public, hash-pinned team bridge. The labels and mirrored grid also matched their published hashes.

That confirms consistency with the bridge—not independent authenticity against DrivenData. Band descriptions in the mirror are unverified and may be inaccurate; official metadata must be checked before scientific use. The competition data page requires login. The large files are kept in an ignored cache rather than the repository.
