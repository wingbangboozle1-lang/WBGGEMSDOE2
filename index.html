01 / HISTORICAL LINEAGE

Why did the B2 file stand out?

On the pinned owner mirrors, its 37,654 dots are a strict subset of the 40,199-dot R1 file, itself a subset of the 44,090-dot D2.8 file. Dots near the known-fault catalogue were removed in successive steps. That can improve the distance-weighted precision/recall trade-off, but hidden labels and a score receipt are unavailable, so causation and the claimed score are not proven.
