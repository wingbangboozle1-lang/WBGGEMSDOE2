### 2.2 Three Quantitative Reasons `GEMSDOE32` (`0.2778`) Won
1. **300 m Kernel Packing Economy (`0.1922 → 0.2477 → 0.2600`):** Under the official Distance-Weighted Tversky Index ($\alpha = 0.2, \beta = 0.8$, 300 m linear triangular kernel $\max(0, 1 - d/300\text{ m})$), a single isolated dot covers a 25-pixel disk of kernel mass `9.38` while paying `1.0` false-positive pixel if it misses (`0.2` weight in denominator). Continuous lines (`H19-5`, `121,131` px) or `1.5 px` dots (`60,069` px) stack 3–5 dots inside the same 300 m radius, multiplying `FP_w` without raising `max`-kernel `TP_w`. Spacing at `2.8 px` cut `FP_w` by `26.6%` (`0.2477 → 0.2600`).
2. **200 m Catalogue-Collar Pruning (`0.2600 → 0.2778`, `+0.0178`):** Organizer evaluation pixel-masks `existing_faults.tif`. Unmapped faults rarely sit parallel `100–200 m` (`1–2 px`) beside an already-digitized fault trace; removing `D28`'s `6,436` collar dots (`14.60% → 0.00%`) eliminated pure false-positive drag in `0.2 * FP_w` with virtually zero `TP_w` loss.
3. **Preserved Distal Piedmont Recall via Spatial-OOF Scoring (`frac_gt_32px = 34.01%`, `C_pied = 0.0888`):** Unlike later Oct 25–26 in-sample PU-HGBR waves (`GEMSDOE28 s21-puhgb = 0.0206`, `GEMSDOE31 pgr31 = 0.0176`, `GEMSDOE24 gvc1 = 0.0859`) where `96%+` of dots collapsed onto steep bedrock range fronts (`frac_gt_32px < 4%`), `GEMSDOE32` placed **one-third of its entire budget (`12,806` dots) more than `3.2 km` from mapped faults** across concealed piedmont fans and intrabasin structures.

## 5. Irregularities Flagged for Manual Review

1. **Public Leaderboard vs Prompt Figure Drift:** The prompt references `0.3195` (`74GEMSDOE` user-reported ledger high) and `0.4413` (`USER-SUPPLIED` earlier leaderboard high), whereas the repository's dated public leaderboard snapshot (`2026-10-27T17:21:43Z`) recorded `0.4544`. None of `0.2778`, `0.3195`, `0.4413`, or `0.4544` has an organizer submission-receipt screenshot checked into Git; all are labeled accordingly (`USER-REPORTED` vs `STALE PUBLIC-LEADERBOARD SNAPSHOT`).
2. **Band 6 (`tc`) Naming Conflict Across Repos:** Official band descriptions label Band 6 `tc` (radiometric Total Count, strictly positive `[7.64, 29.02]` across `5,167,373` pixels), whereas several sibling scripts treated Band 6 as magnetic tilt angle. `WBG2-MSPTCD` and `WBG2-DPHS` compute genuine Miller & Singh (1994) potential-field tilt angles from magnetic/gravity spatial derivatives.
3. **Outside-Footprint `NaN` Portal Trap (`"Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]"`):** Out of `467` audited fleet GeoTIFFs, **189 files** store `NaN` outside the valid polygon (`-nan.tif` / `-nanoutside.tif`), which triggers strict whole-array `[0, 1]` rejection on DrivenData. Always emit and verify `-zeros.tif` (`nodata = None`, `0.0` outside footprint, `12,279,160` finite `float32` pixels).
4. **Prior-Gated External Cache Excluded (`dem1m_high_prior_openness_lrm.npz`):** Byte inspection showed `dem1m_high_prior_openness_lrm.npz` covers only `1.4%` (`169,243` pixels) of the grid gated by an external fault prior, and GDR CSVs contain a `dist_known_fault_px` column. Both were excluded/stripped before feature construction to preserve strict zero-catalogue integrity (`max canary AUC = 0.61795`).
5. **Hide-and-Recover vs Concealed-Piedmont Domain Shift:** `existing_faults.tif` is heavily enriched in steep range-front faults (`det_elev_slope > 10°`). Full-map in-sample PU-HGBR refitting (`0.55` blend in `DPHS-EXP3` packaging) concentrates `96.36%` of dots inside `2..32 px` range fronts (`frac_gt_32px = 3.64%`), triggering `>70%` overlap with 45 fleet range-front rasters and lowering out-of-sample `EMPIRICAL-ESTIMATE` to `0.0713`. Next-session runs must emit **100% spatial-OOF regime-calibrated surfaces** enforcing `30%–40%` distal piedmont (`>32 px`) budget allocation.
