GEMSDOE32 · B2 analysis

What we can—and cannot—infer.

Observed in the raster bytes

GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 have identical in-footprint predictions. GEMSDOE32 B2 is a strict subset of GEMSDOE31 R1, which is a strict subset of GEMSDOE30 D2.8. In the pinned mirrors, the first pruning removes 3,891 dots within one pixel of the known catalogue; the second removes 2,545 dots within two pixels. GEMSDOE36 contains B2 plus 1,200 dots. These are geometry observations, not causal proof.

A plausible mechanism, not a forecast

The organizer's distance-weighted Tversky metric trades 300 m proximity credit against false-positive mass and false negatives. Pruning may help if the removed dots were low-yield, but the hidden test labels are unavailable. Known faults are excluded pixel-exactly; nearby predictions are not free. We cannot infer hidden TP/FP counts, prove why the file scored as claimed, or honestly predict a higher score.

Submission guide

Exactly how to submit—
once a file is approved.

These are future instructions. They do not authorize today's negative result or the historical research downloads.

  1. Confirm the gates and selector

    Use a newly generated, hash-bound TIFF only after literal registry checks before placement and on final dots, feature leakage canaries, a valid paired holdout improvement, format validation and a separate selector decision. The selector must also check the current portal allowance. No such file exists here today.

  2. Check the exact GeoTIFF bytes

    The official format requires a single float32 band, the template's EPSG:32611 CRS, resolution, bounds and transform, valid values in [0, 1], and null/NaN outside the footprint. The local format checker tests the pinned grid, finite [0, 1] values, footprint mask and standalone file, but requires an authenticated reference. Its conservative all-finite policy and a local PASS do not establish portal acceptance or authorize submission.

  3. Investigate the reported range error

    The earlier file and portal validator are unavailable, so its cause is unknown. Some owner mirrors use NaN outside; a zero-filled exterior can pass an all-finite range check but may conflict with the official null-outside wording. Do not assume either encoding is accepted. Use the shared writer and its diagnostics, and retain a portal receipt for any approved file.

  4. Submit via DrivenData

    After the separate selector approves an exact SHA-256 and the portal shows an available slot, sign in, choose the approved TIFF under “File to submit,” enter its short identifying note (at most 140 characters under this project's protocol), and create the submission. Record the exact filename, hash, note, time and organizer receipt. Follow the competition website's current limit; the portal is not accessible in this environment.

Limitations & next steps

What must happen next.

1. Resolve the literal rule

The registered spacing-five calibration lattice is a real entry in the pinned shared registry. It forces a minimum 82.34% overlap for any 37,654-dot footprint subset. Any change to the registry or threshold needs explicit authorization; exempting it silently would violate this run's protocol. A lower dot budget is not automatically scientifically valid or unique.

2. Restore and validate inputs

The checkout did not contain the shared prepared feature stack or authenticated competition files. A raw owner mirror is identified, but its hashes do not authenticate the organizer's files. The DrivenData data page requires login; raw external hosts are blocked from this sandbox. Neither GPU availability nor a complete training pipeline has been verified.

3. Fix the shared registry grid check

A synthetic test showed the pinned shared novelty checker can accept a registry TIFF with an unrelated CRS and transform when its array shape and values match. Our witness audit independently checked its grid, so this does not invalidate the bound. The defect was reported to the shared template; we cannot push a fix to that repository and have not forked it. Read the reproducible diagnostic ↗

4. Run a new, preregistered campaign

Obtain the actual assigned method; the provisional ideas are not an authorization to choose a lane. Use the shared template's cached feature stack, evaluator and writer without a private fork. If the rule becomes feasible and the shared checker is fixed, perform each feature's leakage canary, whole-segment buffered hide-and-recover, pixel-exact masking of visible faults and a pooled 300 m triangular-kernel DTI with a paired spatial confidence interval. Beat a same-protocol incumbent before a selector considers a slot. The provisional hypothesis and three alternatives are recorded in the preregistration; none was tested here.
