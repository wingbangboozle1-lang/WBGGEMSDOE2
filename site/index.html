INTERPRETATION 04 / WHAT WE KNOW

Why did B2
look promising?

The B2 historical mask is exactly a catalogue-distance-pruned subset of the GEMSDOE25/30 dotted mask on the mirrored grid. Fewer low-yield dots can reduce false-positive mass while the metric still gives nearby predictions partial credit.

That mechanism is plausible—not proof of causation. Hidden truth and authenticated per-file receipts are unavailable, so there is no defensible estimate that a new file will beat it.
