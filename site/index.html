A RESOLVED CONTRADICTION 02 / PROTOCOL

The space-filling controls cannot be treated as "dots".

A registered stride-5 lattice covers 99.87% of the footprint within 3 px and a registered continuous prior is positive everywhere — so a literal dot test would reject every candidate, even the 0.2778 champion (0.9992 lattice overlap). We made an explicit, reviewable policy decision: continuous/space-filling controls are compared by the rule's surface rank-correlation branch (|ρ|≤0.22, pass), while the 3 px dot branch applies to discrete submissions (≤29%, pass). INTERPRETATION 04 / WHAT WE KNOW

Why did B2
look promising?

The B2 historical mask is exactly a catalogue-distance-pruned subset of the GEMSDOE25/30 dotted mask on the mirrored grid. Fewer low-yield dots can reduce false-positive mass while the metric still gives nearby predictions partial credit.

That mechanism is plausible—not proof of causation. Hidden truth and authenticated per-file receipts are unavailable, so there is no defensible estimate that a new file will beat it.
