Concurrent distal piedmont hypothesis — research only

Decision: NEGATIVE / DO NOT SUBMIT. A separate session placed a 6,000-dot raster on main. Its N5 method description was self-reported; the governing project brief contains no explicit assignment, and this review did not reconstruct the generator or pre-placement surface. The independent byte audit does not constitute a scientific experiment.

Proposed mechanism and confounders

The reported concept uses distance from mapped faults to explore covered piedmonts where small scarps might not have been mapped, with reported topographic and conductive-base proxies plus road deconfounding. A fault could produce a coherent offset in relief or subsurface properties; a mapped-fault distance mask by itself is not evidence of a fault. Lake Lahontan shorelines, road embankments and erosional channels are non-fault mimics. Geological contacts, survey boundaries and uneven acquisition geometry are additional confounders. Feature provenance, physical units and whether every claimed layer was actually used have not been independently established. A mask chosen to avoid a registry lattice can favour footprint edges rather than geology.

The suggested layers are the owner-mirrored 19-band stack and fault-distance features. The stack is hash-pinned, not authenticated as organizer data. Official sources to investigate include USGS 3DEP, USGS GeoDAWN, DOE INGENIOUS GDR 1391 and Census TIGER/Line. Their fitness, coverage, rights and transformations for this specific candidate remain unverified. No external layer is asserted necessary or already validated.

Future investigation, not authorization

Any future experiment requires an explicitly assigned single-method lane, a clarified continuous-prior rule, an exhaustive pinned registry and the shared-template pre-placement check before placement. Then use fold-visible catalogue features, whole-segment buffered holdout, pixel-exact known-fault masking, individual leakage canaries and the same evaluator/incumbent. Expected runtime and added data cost are not measured. A separate selector must authorize any competition slot. These conditions are not currently met.

The original N1–N4 ranked proposals, mechanisms, non-fault mimics, costs and source limitations remain in research/hypotheses.md.
