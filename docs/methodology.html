Official metric · careful interpretation

Coverage is not truth.

A geometry calculation cannot establish a hidden-label score or override the release gate.

Derived metric identity

With N truth pixels and official weighted TP = T, FN = N − T. Hence DTI = T / ((1−β)T + αF + βN + ε), with α = 0.2, β = 0.8 and a triangular 300 m kernel. T ≤ N. The maximum per-truth probability-weighted credit prevents repeated predictions from accumulating unlimited TP.

Removing a dot can reduce FP, but can also reduce coverage of previously uncovered truth. Per-unit benefit is not a universal constant; it depends on the entire prediction, truth, kernel interactions and masking. Kernel mass or assumed truth counts cannot supply those missing facts.

The H3 sharp-loss/density projection and DZ-16 empirical estimate are historical diagnostics, not validated forecasts. Their apparent fit on related rasters cannot establish a new file's score.

Checks that are not interchangeable

  • Format read-back checks bytes, grid, range and mask; not hidden-fault skill or portal acceptance.
  • Registry checks reject copied/redundant predictions; a different filename, SHA or package is insufficient. An incomplete registry cannot certify clearance.
  • Buffered whole-segment HOLDOUT-DTI with paired spatial CI compares the candidate with a same-fold incumbent. It is not a public/private leaderboard score.
  • Single-feature leakage canaries are necessary warnings, not proof that leakage is absent.
