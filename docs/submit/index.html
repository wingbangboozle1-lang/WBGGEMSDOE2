Exactly how a future approved file is submitted

Do not upload any current file. After a future candidate passes every gate and the separate selector approves a slot: open the official competition, choose New submission, select the exact SHA-256-verified single-band .tif (or ZIP containing just that TIFF), and enter its method/version note of at most 140 characters. Retain the organizer receipt linking the filename and result. File name and note for a new submission: not issued.

The reported “[0,1]” rejection cannot be diagnosed without the rejected bytes. The checker reports NaN, infinity, below-zero, above-one and masked-footprint counts separately. H3-PACK's “zeros” filename actually has NaN outside the footprint. Its local footprint pass does not prove portal acceptance. The authenticated official sample governs outside-footprint encoding.

Why B2 may have done better — not a causal proof

Reopened, hash-checked owner mirrors show D2.8 has 44,090 dots and B2 has 37,654. B2 removes every D2.8 dot at distance ≤2 pixels from the catalogue. GEMSDOE36 then adds 1,200 dots. Sparse placement can reduce false-positive mass while preserving nearby truth coverage under the triangular kernel, but can also remove useful coverage. Without hidden truth and per-file receipts, we cannot say why the user-reported 0.2778 occurred or promise a higher result. A median catalogue distance is not a mandatory exclusion buffer.

Why there is no score estimate

The old DZ-16 regression has negative out-of-sample R² on the nine focus sites. H3-PACK extrapolates a small, related family and confounds count with packing. Neither is a calibrated prediction interval or a substitute for a matched spatial holdout. Their old projections are withdrawn as decision inputs.

Required automated gates

  1. Explicit assigned method lane and authenticated inputs; cached shared features, evaluator and writer.
  2. Complete current registry; pre-placement surface Spearman ≤0.90 and dot overlap ≤70% within three pixels, repeated on final dots. Stop on the first failure; never replace the rule with enrichment or Pearson correlation.
  3. Whole-segment buffered hide-and-recover; catalogue features from visible faults only and exact visible-pixel masking. Feature-alone AUC above 0.90 is a leakage alarm.
  4. Pooled HOLDOUT-DTI with α=0.2, β=0.8 and 300 m triangular kernel; evaluator version, withheld-positive count, spatial 95% CI and matched incumbent comparison. A catalogue holdout is a proxy, not the leaderboard, but hidden organizer truth is not required to run this proxy.
  5. Strict saved-file validation: one float32 band, [0,1], no masked or nonfinite footprint cells, exact CRS/shape/transform; reference provenance and SHA-256 recorded. No silent clipping.
  6. Only a separate selector can choose a real slot after a compliant holdout win and all other gates; verify quota on the authenticated submission page.
