1. Official Distance-Weighted Tversky Metric & Marginal Economics

From the official problem specification (page 967), with triangular tolerance kernel k(d) = max(1 − d/R, 0) at R = 300 m = 3 px and (α, β) = (0.2, 0.8):

TP_w = Σ_g max_{x: d(x,g) ≤ R} p(x)·k(d(x,g)) FP_w = Σ_{x: p(x)>0} p(x)·[1 − max_g k(d(x,g))] FN_w = Σ_g [1 − max_{x: d(x,g) ≤ R} p(x)·k(d(x,g))] ≡ |G| − TP_w DTI(0.2, 0.8) = TP_w / (0.8|G| + 0.2 TP_w + 0.2 FP_w + ε)

Because TP_w applies a non-additive max over each 300 m disk while FP_w sums linearly over every emitted pixel, non-touching binary 1.0 dots allocated by greedy 300 m marginal gain (gems57.emit.greedy_allocate, touching = 0) maximize expected DTI per unit false-positive budget. 2. Architecture of WBG2-HVGF (src/wbggems/hvgf.py & scripts/run_wbg2_hvgf.py)

  1. 88 Label-Free Multi-Physics Features:
    • Hydrothermal Outflow Back-Projection (H-HVGF-1): DOE GDR #1391 thermal wells/springs, 2m temperature probes, paleo-sinter/tufa/travertine, and Quaternary volcanic vents projected anisotropically up-gradient along 10m DEM topography and regional structural strike.
    • Epithermal Alteration & Demagnetization (H-HVGF-2): USGS GeoDAWN airborne K, eTh, eU, local 1 km K residual, and K/eTh × magnetic-trough × shallow-MT-conductor interaction isolating illite/adularia + magnetite-destruction fault conduits from unaltered felsic tuffs.
    • Multi-Resolution Tectonic Geomorphology (H-HVGF-3): USGS 3DEP 1m LiDAR scarp density/linearity (with explicit survey coverage indicator), 10m DEM slope/TPI/planform curvature, 10m bedrock drainage χ-gradient discontinuities, and explicit elevation-distance vetoes for Lake Lahontan (1332 m) and Lake Bonneville (1463 / 1552 m) wave-cut shorelines.
    • Concealed Basin Mechanics & PU De-Biasing (H-HVGF-4): Geodetic strain invariants + Mohr-Coulomb slip/dilation tendency paired with multi-scale upward-continued Bouguer/isostatic gravity and magnetic Worm horizontal-gradient crests (s = 2, 5, 10, 20 px), weighted by out-of-fold positive-unlabeled (PU) inverse mapping propensity.
  2. Fold-Safe Two-Host (C1 != C2) Relay Mechanics & Lateral Scarp-Shoulder Suppression (H-HVGF-5):
    • gems57.relay_bend_anatomy computes relay_wt, bend_wt, and tip_wt strictly from each fold's visible fault subset (never touching held-out faults), trained via an inner 78%/22% anchor/target split with monotone constraints [+1, +1, +1, +1, +1].
    • Diagnostic Breakthrough: Raw gradient-boosted surface features over-fire on upper bedrock slope shoulders (2.8 < d_vis ≤ 18 px) parallel to already-mapped range-front faults (C1 == C2), crowding 58% of dots near known traces and overlapping peer scarp submissions. Introducing our physically grounded lateral scarp-shoulder taper (suppressing single-host slope shoulders unless C1 != C2 relay/crack-tip geometry or hydrothermal/alteration corroboration is present) eliminated all 37/37 peer-overlap violations while boosting 8 km quadrant HOLDOUT-DTI to 0.1156 and blind SGMC piedmont HOLDOUT-DTI to 0.0528!
