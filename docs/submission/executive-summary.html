Why B2 might have improved the historical claim

MEASURED: GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 have identical decoded predictions in the footprint despite different file hashes. B2 is D2.8 after deleting 6,436 dots within two pixels of the mirrored catalogue: 44,090 → 37,654. GEMSDOE36 adds 1,200 dots to B2 and removes none.

DERIVED from the official metric: FN = N − TP and DTI = TP / (0.2·TP + 0.2·FP + 0.8·N + ε). A removed dot helps only if its lost truth coverage is outweighed by the reduced false-positive mass. TP is bounded by N; geometric kernel coverage is not truth coverage. The direction of the USER-REPORTED change from 0.2600 to 0.2778 is plausible, but no hidden labels or receipt establishes its cause.
