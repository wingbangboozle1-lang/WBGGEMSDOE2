Flagged Irregularities Verified From Raw Bytes

  1. Legacy wbg3-h3pack-34k-...-zeros.tif contained 7,111,787 NaN pixels despite -zeros.tif in its filename: Caused DrivenData's "Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]" error. Fixed upstream in src/wbg3/validate.py and scripts/write_h3_submission.py; historical file marked DO NOT SUBMIT.
  2. labels.tif in fleet mirrors is byte-identical to existing_faults.tif (SHA-256 7ba308cc…ae4093): Any metric computed naively against un-split labels.tif measures agreement with the visible catalogue rather than hidden faults (e.g. gems79-tilt-ring-100000dots scored 0.5578 on visible-catalogue proxy and 0.0005 user-reported live). All WBG2-HVGF evaluations strictly withhold spatial blocks/components and recompute every structural feature from visible faults only.
  3. All nine focus-site rasters share 83%–100% 3-px overlap on one parent surface, and none carries an organizer receipt: GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 have identical positive pixel sets; GEMSDOE32 (submissions_manifest.json) records "receipt": null. Every reported peer figure is therefore labeled USER-REPORTED.
  4. Literal 3-px rule degeneracy on blanket registry artifacts: 17GEMSDOE is positive on 100% of the footprint and 13GEMSDOE's stride-5 lattice covers 99.96% of the footprint within 3 px, flagging every 34k–61k submission in competition history. Both literal and normalized enrichment results are published side-by-side.
