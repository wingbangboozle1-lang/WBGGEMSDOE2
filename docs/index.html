Why the reported leader stands out

01
The lineage is exact.

GEMSDOE32 B2 equals the GEMSDOE25 D2.8 dots after removing 6,436 dots within 2 pixels of the mirrored fault catalogue.

02
The metric rewards useful coverage.

A 300 m triangular kernel gives nearby predictions partial credit; thinning can reduce false-positive mass while retaining some coverage.

03
Cause is still uncertain.

The hidden labels and submission receipts are unavailable. The reported score and the reason for it cannot be independently confirmed.

Registry stop witness

100% overlap,
by construction.

A registered continuous probability surface is positive across every mirror-derived footprint cell. The shared literal gate treats every positive cell as support.
