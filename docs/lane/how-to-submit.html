Why a submission is blocked

  • No explicitly assigned method lane in the current research ledger.
  • Current checkout is missing authenticated competition rasters, the derived cache and pinned shared evaluator required for holdout/canary validation.
  • The registry audit is incomplete and the literal three-pixel duplicate gate has decisive witnesses; dense-prior interpretation is unresolved and no exemption is authorized.
  • No separate selector approval or organizer receipt exists. A format check alone is not enough.

Submission workflow once a future file passes every gate

  1. Download the candidate only from an explicitly selector-authorized release page.
  2. Check the exact filename and SHA-256 in its run card.
  3. Log into DrivenData and open the official submission page.
  4. Choose New submission, upload a single-band GeoTIFF or a ZIP containing one GeoTIFF, and paste the release note (at most 140 characters).
  5. After portal acceptance, archive the receipt and label the score ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED. A local validator cannot certify portal acceptance.

Do not upload any of the historical files linked by the nine-site table under the current negative decision.

Range error

DrivenData requires predicted values in [0,1]. A future writer should validate every pixel the portal reads, including masked/outside-footprint cells, against the official sample format. This repository has not identified the exact previously uploaded bytes, so the earlier user error cannot be attributed with certainty to a specific TIFF. Do not assume a zero-outside twin is accepted until the portal confirms it.
