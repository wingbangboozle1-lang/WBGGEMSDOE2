03 / If a future candidate passes

Submission checklist

01

Check the automated decision first.

Obtain an authorized interpretation of the registry rule and a named method lane. Require visible-only feature construction, leakage canaries, a paired spatial holdout win over the strongest comparable baseline, and complete uniqueness and format checks. Promotion is a separate selector step.

02

Download only the newly approved file.

When available, download the single-band GeoTIFF or a ZIP containing exactly one GeoTIFF. Confirm the recorded SHA-256 and unique name. Today there is no such download.

03

Verify format against the organizer's sample.

The official requirements include float32 probabilities in [0,1], EPSG:32611, 100 m pixels, matching bounds/transform/shape, and null or NaN outside bounds. The shared writer rejects invalid values instead of silently clipping them. A locally all-finite output is a precaution, not evidence of organizer acceptance.

04

Use the DrivenData submission form.

After signing in and checking the actual weekly quota on the submission page, choose “Make new submission,” attach the approved file, add its short identifying note, and submit. Save the organizer receipt and score. No upload is made by this site.

04 / Troubleshooting

About the [0,1] error.

Every prediction inside the valid footprint must be finite and between zero and one. Some historical files contain outside-footprint NaNs; those may be relevant to a range error, but the rejected file and portal validator are unavailable, so the actual cause has not been established. Do not silently clip bad predictions. Use the shared, strict saved-byte validator, compare the exact organizer template, and retain the portal's receipt.

Consult the official format, rules, and data page (login required). The organizer may update their rules or validator.
