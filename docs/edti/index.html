1. The question, answered: why did 0.2778 happen?

The official metric is DTI = T / (T + 0.2·F + 0.8·N) with FN ≡ N − T exactly (src/wbg2/metric.py, re-derived from the organizer definitions and brute-force checked in tests/test_wbg4_algebra.py). Writing c = T/N (weighted coverage of the truth set) and f = F/N (false-positive mass per truth cell) gives the single identity that explains the whole leaderboard:

DTI = c / (c + 0.8 + 0.2·f) => c = DTI·(0.8 + 0.2·f) / (1 − DTI)

Because f is not identifiable from a score, a reported value pins coverage only to a band. For the values the brief quotes, with f allowed over [0, 0.6]:

0.2778 is a coverage number, not an accuracy number. DTI at that level is recall-dominated: β = 0.8 prices a missed truth cell at four times a false positive, so the winning move is to convert a good surface into sparse, high-value dots rather than to emit a probability map. That is measured, not asserted — see §4.

Result: all 72 ordered pairs exceed the brief's 0.70 duplicate stop; 64 of 72 exceed 0.90; the maximum is 1.0. GEMSDOE28 and GEMSDOE31 decode to the same 40,199 dots; GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 decode to identical in-footprint arrays (44,090 dots, different GeoTIFF packaging — this reproduces the repository's earlier claim exactly); GEMSDOE32's 37,654 dots are within 3 px of every other site's dots, i.e. a pruned subset of the same emission. The nine-site comparison is therefore not a comparison of methods, and the 0.2449–0.2778 spread is dispersion inside one duplicated family.

This is also the honest answer to "which of the nine should I submit": none of them is a distinct method, so choosing between them is choosing between near-identical arrays, and the highest-reported one (0.2778) carries no verifiable advantage over 0.2708.

3. So can anything beat 0.2778?

To beat it you must lift weighted coverage above ~0.32 while keeping f small. Three routes exist and only one is testable offline:

  • Better emission from the same surface — cheap, measurable, and the only lever this sandbox can validate. Phase 3 delivers it.
  • A better hazard surface — needs features this sandbox cannot reach (see §6). The public fleet has ~100 scored attempts spanning 0.0000–0.2778 with no file above 0.28, which is evidence of a family ceiling.
  • Coverage of genuinely uncatalogued structures — plausible, but the fleet's away-from-catalogue attempts scored 0.00–0.10, so it is high risk until measured on real test truth.

Three findings, all measured:

The candidate is deliberately in the same emission family as the fleet (sparse binary dots near structural evidence) but its dots are placed by a different surface: the closest of the nine still only contains 35–47 % of them. That is the intended trade — same family, different membership.

Irregularity flagged — the literal rule is vacuous. The 13GEMSDOE raster is a stride-5 lattice (all positives at row ≡ col ≡ 0 mod 5, 206,895 cells). An exact measurement gives 5,160,724 of 5,167,373 footprint cells — 0.998713 — lying within 3 px of a lattice point, reproducing the repository's figure to the last digit. Because that single raster is within 3 px of 99.87 % of the whole map, every non-empty submission overlaps it by ≥ 99.87 % unless its dots are confined to the 6,649 uncovered cells. Under a literal reading of the brief, therefore, no submission can ever pass while that lattice is in the registry — including the nine files the brief itself recommends. The audit therefore applies the rule's stated purpose (detect drift into a rival method) to method-bearing emissions, and reports the lattice separately as a covering set. This is a policy question for the requester, and it is flagged rather than resolved unilaterally.

6. Limits — what this cannot do

  • No organizer truth. Public and private test labels are not in this sandbox and drivendata.org is unreachable. Every offline number is measured against the training catalogue, which is a different population from the scored "new expert-labelled faults". A holdout that recovers withheld catalogue faults is systematically biased toward methods that hug the catalogue — i.e. toward the very family that is capped at 0.28.
  • No better external data reachable. The sandbox network allows GitHub and PyPI only. A genuinely new hazard surface needs at least one of: USGS Quaternary Fault and Fold Database slip rates, EarthScope/UNAVCO GNSS strain rates, ANSS ComCat relocated seismicity, or NBMG 1:250k geology. Each is free and official; none is retrievable here. This is the single highest-value unblock for the next session.
  • The empirical score model is not usable for this candidate. data_public/score_model.json reports LOSO R² = 0.28 and, on the nine held-out focus sites, R² = −0.15 with MAE 0.075. It is retained as a diagnostic and deliberately not used to produce the 0.20–0.30 projection, which is instead anchored on the emission-family band plus the EXP-2 holdout optimum.
  • n = 2 folds. No confidence interval is claimed for any holdout figure in this report.
  • One registry raster was skipped (a97c7b2a513225c9.tif, shape 32×48 — a thumbnail, not a submission-grid raster). It is recorded with the error in evidence/wbg4_edti_uniqueness.json rather than silently dropped.

10. Irregularities flagged for review

  • The literal 70 % / 0.90 duplicate rule cannot be satisfied by any non-empty submission while the 13GEMSDOE stride-5 lattice (covering fraction 0.998713) is counted as a registry raster. Needs a policy decision: does the rule apply to covering sets and continuous priors, or only to method-bearing emissions? (§5)
  • All nine focus sites' top files are duplicates of one another under that same rule (72/72 pairs > 0.70). The brief's premise that these are nine independent results does not hold on the bytes. (§2)
  • Dot counts disagree with the repository's earlier table for GEMSDOE28 and GEMSDOE31 (measured here: 40,199 and 40,199; earlier record: 37,660 and 40,199). The two files are the same array, so the earlier split appears to be a transcription artefact. (§2)
  • Two rasters whose scores are blank are format-identical to scored ones — the repository's own forensics record this, and it means the blank scores cannot be blamed on a format failure.
  • Hazard probabilities are not calibrated (total mass 242,609 against 60,988 label cells, because training negatives were subsampled). Placement uses ranks only, so the emission is unaffected, but no probability value in this hazard may be read as a real frequency.
