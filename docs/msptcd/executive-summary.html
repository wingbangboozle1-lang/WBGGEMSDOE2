1. GEMSDOE32 B2 (0.2778 USER-REPORTED): measured lineage, not a causal score explanation

  • Exact pixel-set lineage: Across all 5,167,373 in-footprint cells, GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 decode to the same 44,090 dots (both scores are USER-REPORTED at 0.2600). GEMSDOE32 has 37,654 dots and a USER-REPORTED score of 0.2778; its decoded set equals the GEMSDOE25/30 set minus 6,436 dots within <= 2 px of the mirrored existing_faults.tif, with no added pixels. This is a reproducible byte-level lineage observation, not proof of the exact uploaded file or of why its score differed.
  • Metric suggests a mechanism, but hidden truth is unavailable: Under the official distance-weighted Tversky objective, reducing redundant false-positive mass could help. The amount of hidden truth intersected by the removed dots is unknown here, so the lineage comparison cannot establish a causal score gain or a new-score forecast.
  • Reported private-holdout/public-score mismatch: A separate parallel artifact was source-reported at 0.1321 on a private, unbuffered evaluator and later listed with a USER-REPORTED public DTI of 0.0725. This is not an organizer receipt and is not a controlled comparison; it motivates using the shared buffered holdout protocol but does not validate a leaderboard model.
  • Does MSPTCD beat the public leaderboard? Not established. The WBG2-MSPTCD result is HOLDOUT-DTI under a local evaluator, not a projection or live score. Its improvement over a matched control cannot be compared numerically to the public leaderboard or GEMSDOE32's USER-REPORTED score.

The separate detached-splay single-seed diagnostic does not have a spatial-bootstrap 95% CI, so it is intentionally excluded from score reporting here; it is not presented as HOLDOUT-DTI.

2. Critical Metadata Discrepancy Resolved: Band 6 (tc) Is Positive Radiometric Total Counts

Earlier literature/repo summaries labeled Band 6 as “Tilt angle or total curvature”, whereas the official DrivenData problem page lists total airborne gamma-ray radiometric counts (tc) alongside K, U, and Th. Direct measurement over all 5,167,373 finite in-footprint cells of training_features.tif settles it unambiguously:

  • Band 6 (tc) quantiles: q01 = 7.637, median = 18.490, q99 = 29.020, negative pixels = 0.
  • A Miller & Singh (1994) magnetic tilt angle arctan(VDR / THDR) spans [-π/2, +π/2] ≈ [-1.57, +1.57] rad with ~50% negative values. Band 6 is strictly positive airborne radiometric count rate; true potential-field tilt derivatives had never been computed in raw bands and are explicitly derived from first principles in src/wbggems/msptcd.py.
