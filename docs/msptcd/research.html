H1 · Rank 1 (Tested in EXP-1/2/3)

Multi-Scale Potential-Field Tilt-Gradient & Dip-Shifted Gravity–Magnetic–Basement Concordance

Exact layers: bouguer_gravity_anomaly (B4), magnetic_anomaly (B5), depth_to_base_surf (B9)

Physical signature: Depth-invariant Miller & Singh (1994) tilt horizontal-gradient ridges at σ ∈ {300, 800, 2000} m aligned with dip-tolerant (0–600 m lateral offset) Bouguer–magnetic gradient concordance across basement-depth steps.

Why it catches uncatalogued faults: Recovers blind intrabasin horst/graben bounding faults buried beneath unscarped quaternary alluvial fans.

Named non-fault mimics: Unfaulted pluton margins, volcanic/dike contacts, paleochannel edges.

H2 · Rank 2 (Tested in EXP-1/2/3)

Cross-Strike Electrical Conductivity Flank Asymmetry × Heat-Flow Relay Breaches

Exact layers: cond_surf (B8), depth_to_base_surf (B9), heat_flow (B11)

Physical signature: Signed across-strike step asymmetry |μ(+400..1200 m) − μ(−400..−1200 m)| / IQR along structural strike gated by conductive cover thickness and conductive heat-flow gradients.

Why it catches uncatalogued faults: Detects hydrothermally clay-altered or brine-saturated fault-valve conduits sealed at the surface.

Named non-fault mimics: Playa evaporite/clay pans (Carson Sink / Humboldt Sink) — mitigated via topographic concavity & slope gating.
