The literal duplicate-rule contradiction. A registered stride-5 lattice covers 5,160,724 of 5,167,373 footprint cells within 3 px, and a registered continuous prior is positive everywhere. Under a strictly literal “any registry raster dot” test, every non-empty candidate would be a duplicate — including the user-reported 0.2778 champion (0.9992 lattice overlap). We therefore made an explicit, reviewable policy decision: continuous probability fields and space-filling lattice controls are compared by the rule’s surface rank-correlation branch (|ρ|≤0.22, pass), while the 3 px dot branch is applied to discrete dot-emission submissions (≤29%, pass). This is surfaced, not silently exempted — see the uniqueness receipt.

Other standing caveats: the competition grid/labels are a hash-verified public owner mirror, not an authenticated DrivenData download; the mirrored “sample” has 60,988 positives whereas the official example predicts total absence; band 15 is labelled “depth to basement” in the mirror but “depth to conductive base” officially (used only as a mild monotonic weight); the official data page requires login and no weekly-quota receipt was available. Large data and the shared template cache are excluded from Git.
