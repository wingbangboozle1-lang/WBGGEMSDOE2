Method Summary

Hypothesis: Blind geothermal faults concentrate at structural positions near catalogue-adjacent geophysical anomalies. A multi-physics consensus surface weighted by inverse distance to catalogue faults identifies fault-proximal zones missed by ridge-dot patterns.

Mechanism: Consensus surface from magnetic (rtp, tc), gravity (iso_grav_anom), topographic (det_elev_slope), strain (geod_shearrate), and seismic (ieq_n100a15) features at 300 m LoG scale. Binary dots placed in near-fault zones (0-5 px from catalogue) ranked by geophysical surface strength.

Why it finds uncatalogued faults: Targets fault-proximal structural positions rather than continuous ridges, capturing blind fault segments and stepovers that surface mapping misses.

Non-fault mimic: Unfaulted lithologic contacts, intrusive margins, or sedimentary facies changes can produce geophysical edges near catalogue faults.

Difference from prior methods: Historical submissions place dots along linear ridges (~37,654 dots spread along features). This candidate concentrates dots near catalogue faults with geophysical weighting — a fundamentally different spatial pattern (12.96% overlap vs GEMSDOE32 B2, rank corr -0.0042).
