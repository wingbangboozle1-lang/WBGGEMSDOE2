## Evidence, data availability and boundaries

* The [official problem description](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) enumerates the competition bands and says the task is **fault** mapping. A fault is a potential geothermal conduit, not proof of a vent or a commercially viable resource.
* The [USGS GeoDAWN release](https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and) documents high-resolution magnetic/radiometric survey data, different acquisition specifications, flight lines and variable terrain clearance. It cautions that acquisition geometry matters during interpretation. The release is marked CC0.
* [USGS structural-discontinuity research](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/structural-discontinuities-and-their-control-hydrothermal-systems-great-basin-usa) links fault terminations and stepovers to localized stress/strain and many Great Basin hydrothermal systems. It does **not** prove that any proposed raster cell is a fault or geothermal vent.
* The official [GDR INGENIOUS compilation](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391) lists MT, Quaternary faults, shallow temperatures, geothermal features, well/spring chemistry and source licenses. The page and resource links are obtainable through the research fetch tool. The sandbox cannot directly fetch GDR/USGS binary data due its network allowlist; no binary download from those official hosts was verified here. Do not treat external proposals requiring new downloads as operational until actual bytes and licensing/coverage have been checked.
* An owner-hosted public mirror of the nineteen-band stack and sample/fault grids was restored, integrity-pinned and cached locally. Hash equality proves mirror consistency, **not** that these are an authenticated DrivenData download. The cache builder and evaluator/writer are the unchanged pinned shared template. See [data preparation receipt](../data/data_preparation.json) in the repository. Data and caches are ignored by Git and must be restored on a fresh machine.
* The raster's own feature descriptions and a sibling report disagree about the interpretation of band 6 (`tc`). No band-6 transform is proposed until the official metadata and raw values can resolve the discrepancy.
