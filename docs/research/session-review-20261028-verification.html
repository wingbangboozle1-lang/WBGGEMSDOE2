4. Why B2 may have reached 0.2778 — and whether we can beat it

Why (plausible, not proven). The official metric is DTI = T / [(1−β)T + αF + βN + ε] with α=0.2, β=0.8, 300 m triangular kernel. B2 removes 6,436 dots within 2 px of the mapped catalogue from the 44,090-dot D2.8 mask. Removing dots can raise DTI only if the lost false-positive mass ΔF outweighs the lost truth coverage ΔT; the improvement condition is [1−(1−β)s]·ΔT > α·s·ΔF. Both ΔT and ΔF were not measured, so the 0.2778 mechanism is a hypothesis, not a verified decomposition. (An older claim that the removal saved 0.2·ΔFP ≈ 12,074 is withdrawn as impossible: even at one mass unit per removed dot the weighted reduction is ≤ 1,287.2.)

Can we beat it? Unknown, and not currently testable here: - The method lane is UNASSIGNED, so no candidate may be implemented. - No authenticated competition feature stack / labels are present in this checkout (data/ holds only the two small reference mirrors; the 418 MB training stack is not restored). - The best measured candidate in the repository is WBG2-DISTAL natural_oof, HOLDOUT-DTI 0.1770, 95% CI [0.1549, 0.1976], evaluator gems57-pooled-hide-v2, 60,988 withheld positives, 61/61 leakage canaries ≤ 0.6180 — but it also fails the literal all-registry rule (max registry dot overlap 0.9066 > 0.70) for the same blanket-control reason. - The empirical fleet model's point estimate for the archived candidate is 0.1493, 95% PI [0.000, 0.333], external-test RMSE 0.094; it cannot certify anything above the observed 0.2778 and gives no evidence about the 0.33–0.45 leaderboard band. - The organizer-published public leaderboard high observed on 2026-10-27 was 0.4544 (xiaofanhu). This is a public observation, not a per-file receipt.
