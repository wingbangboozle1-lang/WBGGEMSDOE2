Why 0.2778 won — and what we actually tested.

PhD-level, byte-verified analysis. Every number is labelled HOLDOUT-DTI or USER-REPORTED; none is a leaderboard score.

A byte-verified reading of the 0.2778 result

The nine focus-site “best” files are one near-identical family: GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 have identical decoded footprint predictions (44,090 dots); GEMSDOE32 B2 equals that D2.8 raster with all dots within 2 px of the mapped catalogue removed (6,436 pruned → 37,654); GEMSDOE36 adds 1,200 dots back and removes none. Their claims (0.2449–0.2778) are USER-REPORTED, not organizer-confirmed receipts.

Why sparse + pruned can win under DTI (α=0.2, β=0.8, 300 m kernel): false negatives are weighted 4× false positives, so a sparse dotted line along structural ridges keeps partial kernel credit near many possible true traces while keeping the FP term small. Dots on or beside mapped faults earn no credit because the organizer masks known-fault pixels; pruning that halo removes wasted mass. Sparse emission also avoids redundant, overlapping dots that add FP without adding coverage. This explains the ordering 0.2778 (B2) > 0.2750 (B2 + points) > 0.2600 (unpruned D2.8) as a plausible mechanism — not a proven cause, since the private truth and per-dot receipts are unavailable.

Can we beat it? Only an organizer result can answer that. The three leaderboard figures in the brief (0.4413 public lead, 0.3195, 0.2778) are inconsistent, time-varying, user-reported observations of different rounds — not receipts bound to these bytes. No calibrated leaderboard forecast exists, so we deliberately submit a genuinely different prediction (H1) rather than another member of the same family.

Five ranked geological hypotheses

Ranked by expected HOLDOUT-DTI gain ÷ implementation cost. Only rank 1 was implemented and validated on the spatially-blocked holdout before any submission-slot consideration; ranks 2–5 are unmeasured priors, not scores. Free official data sources are named where an idea would need new input.

RANK 1 VALIDATED TOP CANDIDATE

H1 — Multiscale cross-physics lineament coherence (PROMOTED)

Layers: tc, iso_grav_anom, tmi, det_elev, cond_surf, geod_2ndinv (+ depth_to_base_surf prior)

Physical signature: Through-going linear discontinuities whose Gaussian-derivative orientation is coherent across scales (σ=1,2,4 px) AND coincident across independent physics; combined by geometric mean so a single-sensor artifact cannot dominate.

Why it catches a catalogue-missing fault: Buried / blind faults can lack a surface scarp yet still juxtapose density, magnetisation, conductivity and strain — a coincident multi-physics edge survives where a single DEM/topo ridge does not. The depth prior up-weights basin fill where faults are missing from surface mapping.

Non-fault mimic: Aeromagnetic flight-line leveling artifacts; intrusive/dike or lithologic contacts. Mitigated by cross-physics agreement; a deeply buried contact can still be indistinguishable from a blind fault.

Difference from this repo / fleet: New to this repo (uses the restored 19-band stack, not the d2.8 dotted-ridge family). Verified: |ρ|≤0.22, dot overlap ≤0.29 vs registry.

Expected DTI improvement: Validated: HOLDOUT-DTI ~0.0966 (CI 0.0877–0.1051) vs B2 mechanism 0.0039 on the same folds.

Implementation cost / data: Low — all layers already provided; no external download needed.

Promoted mechanism & named non-fault mimic

Six provided edge/gradient layers (magnetic tilt/tc, isostatic gravity anomaly, TMI, detrended elevation, conductivity, geodetic strain invariant) are robust-normalised; for each, a multiscale (sigma=1,2,4 px) Gaussian-derivative edge magnitude is multiplied by cross-scale orientation coherence to isolate through-going linear discontinuities. Rank-normalised layers are combined by GEOMETRIC MEAN (requiring agreement across independent physics) and mildly weighted by depth to conductive base. From this catalogue-independent surface, TWO populations of non-maximum-suppressed peaks are emitted as confidence-1 dots, both using fold-local VISIBLE-fault geometry only: (1) near-catalogue continuation dots within a 2-8 px halo (physics-ranked) and (2) off-catalogue whole-footprint lineament dots. Mapped-fault pixels are excluded by a 2 px collar. Emission hedges the hide-and-recover proxy's near-fault bias while keeping coverage of genuinely-new off-catalogue faults that the leaderboard rewards.

Named non-fault mimic: Aeromagnetic flight-line leveling artifacts and buried intrusive/dike or lithologic contacts — coherent multiscale linear edges in potential fields that are not through-going brittle faults. Mitigation: cross-physics geometric mean; caveat: a deeply buried intrusive contact can remain indistinguishable from a blind fault.

Uniqueness: max surface |ρ| 0.22 (limit 0.90); max final-dot 3px overlap vs real submissions 29% (limit 70%). See the uniqueness receipt and run card.
