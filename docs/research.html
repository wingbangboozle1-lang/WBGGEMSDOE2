Historical shortlist (26 October review lane)

The earlier lane's method_lane status was UNASSIGNED; its rankings were expressed as spatially blocked HOLDOUT-DTI potential, not scores, and no experiment or slot is authorized by that shortlist. It is retained for audit continuity alongside the new 2026-10-27 preregistration above.

  • H1 — Repeated, catchment-normalized cross-drainage deflections
    Ask whether several independent streams change direction or bed gradient consistently on crossing a candidate line, normalized for drainage area and relief; compare with randomized orientations and other lithology boundaries.. Non-fault mimic: Lithological boundaries, terraces, alluvial fans, stream capture, roads and inherited drainage can create repeated deflections.. High: DEM processing and multiple hydrologic controls.
  • H2 — Multi-depth magnetotelluric conductance coherence with uncertainty
    Compare the orientation and location of conductance transitions across separately resolved depth intervals, propagate uncertainty and covariance from the common inversion, and reject apparent continuity in undersampled areas; do not treat the intervals as independent observations.. Non-fault mimic: Clays, saline fluids, lithologic contacts and gridding artefacts also create conductance boundaries; a conductor can be geologically unrelated to a fault.. High: survey resolution, reprojection, independent source overlap and anti-leakage checks.
  • H3 — Cross-flight-line repeatability veto for magnetic boundaries
    Compare independently observed boundary locations and orientations from crossing flight and tie lines; downweight structures that shift with acquisition direction or clearance.. Non-fault mimic: Dikes and unfaulted lithologic contacts are stable across flight directions; sparse crossings or variable clearance can generate false disagreement.. High: raw line downloads, acquisition-geometry reconstruction and coverage checks.
  • H4 — Repeat-pass deformation discontinuity with a pumping control
    Look for persistent, independently repeated displacement gradients across a candidate trace while controlling for pumping, atmospheric artefacts, phase unwrapping and seasonal changes.. Non-fault mimic: Groundwater pumping, recharge, atmospheric delay, landslides, mines, phase-unwrapping errors and radar decorrelation.. High: independent acquisition, atmospheric correction, temporal control and georeferencing.
