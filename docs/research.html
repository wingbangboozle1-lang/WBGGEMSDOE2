Depth-shifted gravity–magnetic discontinuity (H1)

Signed directional gradients at 300/600/1200 m: test whether a magnetic edge displaced along the gravity-edge normal by a bounded depth/dip proxy indicates a concealed fault. Uses iso_grav_anom, rtp, depth_to_base_surf (conductive base, not basement), det_elev.

02 · MEDIUM COST

Conductive-flank asymmetry

Compare conductivity on opposite sides of a depth/gravity step, normalized by local background and depth — lateral flank asymmetry, not conductivity alone.

Non-fault mimic: Clay-rich sediments, saline groundwater, non-faulted contacts.

Non-fault mimic: Drainage incisions, alluvial fans, shorelines, roads and landslides.
